Financial planning services in Singapore

One adviser looks at your whole financial picture, so your retirement, investments, insurance and estate plans work together instead of against each other.

  • Retirement planning

    Work out how much you need, when you can stop working and how to turn CPF, SRS and savings into a dependable monthly income.

  • Investment planning

    A diversified portfolio matched to your goals and risk comfort, with fees you can see. We explain every recommendation in plain English.

The retirement readiness checklist

15 checks for Singaporeans in their 30s, 40s and 50s. Horizon Wealth Planning.

Your retirement income

  • Write down the monthly income you want in retirement, in today's dollars.
  • Check your CPF statement and estimated CPF LIFE payouts on the CPF website.
  • Decide whether topping up your CPF Retirement Account makes sense for you.
  • See whether SRS contributions could lower your income tax this year.
  • Keep six months of expenses in an emergency fund you can reach quickly.

Your investments

  • List every investment account you own and what you pay in fees each year.
  • Check your mix of shares, bonds and cash still suits the years left before you retire.
  • Gather old policies and accounts you have lost track of.
  • Plan how you will turn savings into a steady monthly income.
  • Clear high-interest debt, such as credit cards, before you stop working.

Protection and paperwork

  • Review your hospital cover: MediShield Life and any Integrated Shield plan.
  • Check your critical illness and disability cover against your income.
  • Make a will, or update it after marriage, divorce or children.
  • Make a CPF nomination so your CPF savings go to the people you choose.
  • Set up a Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA) while you are well.

Questions people ask before booking

How much does the first consultation cost?

Nothing. The first 45-minute review is free and there is no obligation. If you decide to work with us, we set out our fees in writing before you commit to anything.

Do I need a lot of money to see a financial planner?

No. Many of our clients start in their twenties and thirties with modest savings. The earlier you plan, the less you need to set aside each month.

What happens in the first meeting?

We talk through your goals, your current savings, CPF, insurance and any worries you have. You leave with a clear picture of where you stand. We do not sell products in the first meeting.

How accurate is the retirement calculator?

It is an illustration based on fixed assumptions: 5% yearly growth, 2.5% inflation and income needed until age 90. Real returns vary, and it does not model CPF LIFE payouts separately. Use it as a starting point, not a plan.

Can you help with CPF and SRS decisions?

Yes. We look at CPF top-ups, CPF LIFE options and whether SRS contributions make sense for your tax bracket as part of your overall retirement plan.

How do you handle my personal data?

We only use your details to reply to you and to send what you asked for. We handle personal data in line with Singapore's PDPA, never sell it, and you can unsubscribe or ask us to delete it at any time.
